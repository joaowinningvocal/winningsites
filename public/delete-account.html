Delete your Winning Ranch account

For the Winning Ranch app by Winning Realty LLC, on Google Play and the App Store.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the Winning Ranch app and log in.
  2. Tap the button with your role at the top right (Owner, Ranch hand, Office…).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type your password and tap Delete my account.

Your account is closed right away and you are signed out.

Can’t open the app?

Ask us to delete it: email Jenna@WinningRanch.com or call (305) 409-9656 from the email or phone on your account. We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number and password.
  • Your access to the app, its chats and your horse’s feed.

What we keep

  • Documents you signed (boarding agreements, waivers), because the law requires us to keep them.
  • Messages you sent in chats with the ranch, shown as from “Deleted account”.
  • For the ranch team: shift hours, kept for payroll records, and the horse updates you posted for owners.
