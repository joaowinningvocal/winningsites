I understand that horses are large, powerful, and unpredictable animals. Risks include being bitten, kicked, stepped on, crushed, dragged, or trampled; being thrown or falling; horses bolting, bucking, rearing, spooking, slipping, or colliding; equipment failure; uneven or slippery footing; weather; and the actions of other people or animals. Serious injury, permanent disability, paralysis, or death may result. Instruction, supervision, and protective equipment cannot eliminate all risks.
