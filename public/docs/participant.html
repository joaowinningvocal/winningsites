1. ACTIVITIES COVERED

2. ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF INHERENT RISKS

I understand that horses are large, powerful, and unpredictable animals. Equine activities involve inherent risks that cannot be eliminated, even with reasonable care, instruction, supervision, and protective equipment. Risks include being bitten, kicked, stepped on, crushed, dragged, or trampled; being thrown or falling; a horse bolting, bucking, rearing, spooking, slipping, or colliding; equipment failure; uneven or slippery footing; weather and environmental conditions; and the actions of other people or animals. Serious injury, permanent disability, paralysis, or death may result.

3. ASSUMPTION OF RISK

I knowingly and voluntarily assume all risks associated with my participation and presence, including known and unknown risks and risks inherent in equine activities. I accept responsibility for my own decisions, actions, conduct, and participation.

4. RELEASE AND WAIVER

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, I release, waive, discharge, and covenant not to sue the Released Parties for claims, damages, losses, costs, or liabilities arising out of or relating to my participation in equine activities or presence on the property. This release expressly includes claims based on the ordinary negligence of a Released Party, including ordinary negligence relating to property conditions, maintenance, horse selection or handling, instruction, supervision, warnings, tack, equipment, or acts and omissions of personnel. This release does not apply to gross negligence, willful or wanton misconduct, intentional injury, or liability that cannot legally be waived.

5. NEVADA EQUINE ACTIVITY LAW

I understand that Nevada Revised Statutes (NRS) 41.519 addresses inherent risks of equine activities and provides statutory protections subject to exceptions. Those exceptions may include certain failures to assess a participant’s ability, known dangerous property conditions, defective equipment, failure to act responsibly, and willful or wanton disregard. This Agreement supplements applicable law and does not waive non-waivable rights or duties.

6. PARTICIPANT SAFETY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Disclose my riding experience, abilities, limitations, and relevant safety information.
  • Wear appropriate footwear and required protective equipment, including a properly fitted riding helmet when required.

  • Immediately report injury, discomfort, unsafe conditions, or concerns.
  • Accept that the Instructor or Company may stop or refuse participation when they reasonably believe it is unsafe.
