Stall board guide

The Stall board shows who is in every stall, today and for the next two weeks. Open it from the dashboard (Stall board tab) or from Stall board on the app's Home screen.

1. Reading the board

The barn is drawn the way it stands: 11 to 20 on the left, 1 to 10 on the right, the aisle in between and the doors at the bottom. Each stall faces the one 10 numbers away (5 faces 15).

  • Green: free. Sand: monthly boarder. Pink: nightly guest. Striped gray: one of our horses. Dark stripes: not in use.
  • The arrows and the date at the top change the day. The calendar under the barn shows the next two weeks; tap any day of a stall to open it.
  • "Boarder" or "Our horse" with no name are the horses from your barn sheet. Tap them and type the name.

2. Putting a horse in a stall

This is how you block a stall out.

  1. Tap the stall.
  2. Tap Add a horse to this stall.
  3. Type the horse's name. Tick Pony if it is one (only ponies go in stall 10).
  4. More horses arriving together? + Another horse adds a row, each with its own stall.
  5. Pick Monthly boarder (leave Leaves empty) or Nightly (arrival and departure day).
  6. Add the owner, up to 3 phone numbers (+ Phone number) and notes like the AM and PM feed.
  7. Tap Add to the board.
To change a horse that's already there (name, notes, phones, dates), tap the stall and then Edit. There's no need to add it again.

3. Making a stall available again

Tap the stall. What you do next depends on why it's taken.

A horse is leaving

Tap Moves out. The stall is free from that day. Nightly stays free up by themselves on their departure day.

EditMoves out Oct 30Remove

It was a mistake, or the stall is actually empty

Tap Remove. Use this for the unnamed horses from the barn sheet too when that stall is really empty.

Only one horse of a group is leaving

Tap Edit and remove that horse's row with its ×. Moves out and Remove apply to the whole group.

It says "Not in use"

Dashboard → Stalls → tap the stall → Status: In use → Save stall.

4. Moving horses between stalls

For when you rotate horses.

  1. Press and hold the horse for about half a second (on a computer, just drag it).
  2. Drag it onto another stall. The stall it will land in gets a red border.
  3. Let go. If there was a horse there, the two swap.

5. Our own horses

Tick One of our own horses when you add them. Their stall still counts as available for rent, because they can go to the other ranch. When a booking lands on one, the stall says:

A new horse in a stall that still has an unnamed horse from the barn sheet simply replaces it.

6. Finding stalls for a group

Horses that arrive together are bonded, so they should stay close. At the top of the board:

  • Stall assistant (when it's turned on): type something like "I have 5 horses coming in Nov 15 - Nov 17". It picks stalls side by side and books them. Each answer has an Undo button.
  • Find stalls yourself: fill in how many horses, how many ponies and the dates, tap Find stalls, then Book these on the option you like.

7. The Horses tab

The Horses tab lists the same horses as the board. A horse you add on the board shows up there, with the owner's name even if they don't have the app yet. Picking a stall in Horses moves the horse on the board, and Left the ranch frees its stall.

That's also where the team posts photos and videos for a horse (Post update).
