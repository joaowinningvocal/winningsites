Booking an event stall

One horse per stall. You pick the stalls, we hold them and confirm.

  1. Pick dates and stalls

    The ranch map shows which stalls are open for your dates, with photos and prices.

  2. Send your request

    We hold your stalls and a team member confirms by phone or email. Every rate includes full care.

  3. Sign waivers, bring your Coggins

    Everyone in your party signs before entering the ranch. A full Coggins is required for every horse.

Questions owners ask

Anything else? Call a team member at (305) 409-9656.

What do I need to bring?

A full Coggins for every horse. Your horse can’t enter the ranch without one. Everyone in your party also needs a signed waiver.

What does the rate include?

Full care. Every rate on the site is for full care, which includes what full care includes.

Can two horses share a stall?

No, it’s one horse per stall. Book one stall for each horse you’re bringing.

Is there room for my trailer or RV?

Yes, on our gravel lot, but spots are limited. For RV parking, trailer parking and dry hookups, contact a team member at (305) 409-9656 to confirm before you book.

Can I see the stall before I book?

Yes. Every stall on the ranch map has its photos, size and price. You’re also welcome to call a team member and come see it.

Will I get updates on my horse?

Yes. Owners with a horse at the ranch get a login to the Winning Ranch app, where the team posts photos and videos of your horse.

Do you charge for blanketing or fly spray?

No. Fly masks on and off, daily fly spray and blankets on and off at your request are part of full care, with no charge per change.

Can I use the PEMF and massage equipment?

Yes, free for boarders. We have the Sport Innovations ProSeries 3 in 1 (PEMF, heat and massage, with hock wraps) and a Magnawave PEMF machine. Ask a team member to set up a session.

What hay is included with board?

Every rate includes full care with hay: hay type and how much.

Can I check on my horse when I’m not there?

Yes. The barn has cameras with audio, so you can see your horse and talk to them. Ask a team member for access. Owners also get photo and video updates from the team in the Winning Ranch app.

How can I pay?

Credit card, Zelle, cash or check.

Do you offer monthly boarding?

Yes, full-service monthly boarding. Contact us to reserve a stall.
