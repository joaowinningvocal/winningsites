Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information Winning Ranch (“we”) collects through the Winning Ranch app and website, how we use it, and the choices you have. Winning Ranch is a horse boarding facility at 5360 W Agate Ave, Las Vegas, NV.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and phone number. Accounts are created by the ranch team for horse owners and staff; there is no public sign-up.
  • Horse and stay details: your horses’ names and breeds, stall assignments, arrival and departure dates, and care notes (for example feeding instructions).
  • Photos and videos: updates the ranch team posts about your horse, and photos you send in chats.
  • Messages: the chats between you and the ranch team.
  • Signed documents: the waivers and agreements you sign, the information you enter in them, your signature, and the date, time, IP address and device type of the signature, kept as a record of the agreement.
  • Booking and contact requests: what you send through the stall booking and contact forms on the website.
  • Location (ranch staff only): when a staff member clocks in or out, the app checks the phone’s location to confirm they are at the ranch. Location is read only at that moment, not tracked in the background, and is never collected from horse owners.

The app also uses the camera and photo library only when you choose to take or attach a photo or video.

How we use it

  • To run the boarding service: assign stalls, care for your horse and keep you updated.
  • To let you and the ranch team message each other and share photos.
  • To keep signed waivers and agreements on file.
  • To track staff hours.
  • To send account invitations, signature requests and similar service emails.

We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers that run the app for us, and only for that purpose:

  • our hosting provider, where the app and its data are stored;
  • our email provider, to deliver service emails;
  • Anthropic, whose AI assistant helps the ranch team plan stall bookings. It processes the booking details the team types in (such as horse and owner names and dates).

We may also disclose information when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep account, horse and message information while you board with us or work at the ranch, and for a reasonable time afterward. Signed waivers and agreements are kept as long as needed for legal and insurance purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or to close your account, by contacting us below. We will respond within 30 days. Some records, such as signed agreements, may be kept where the law requires it.

Security

Passwords are never stored in readable form, connections use HTTPS, and photos, videos, chats and signed documents are only available to the people they belong to and the ranch team.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. A parent or guardian signs on behalf of a minor rider.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
