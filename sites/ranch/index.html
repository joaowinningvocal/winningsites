Booking an event stall

One horse per stall. You pick the stalls, we hold them and confirm.

  1. Pick dates and stalls

    The ranch map shows which stalls are open for your dates, with photos and prices.

  2. Send your request

    We hold your stalls and Jenna confirms by phone or email. Add full care or trailer and RV parking when you book.

  3. Sign waivers, bring your Coggins

    Everyone in your party signs before entering the ranch. A full Coggins is required for every horse.

Good to know before you book

  • Rates shown are for self care. Full care is an add-on.
  • RV parking, trailer parking and dry hookups are limited. Call Jenna at (305) 409-9656 to confirm first.

Questions owners ask

Anything else, call Jenna at (305) 409-9656.

What do I need to bring?

A full Coggins for every horse. Your horse can’t enter the ranch without one. Everyone in your party also needs a signed waiver.

What’s the difference between self care and full care?

Event stall rates are for self care, where you handle feeding and cleaning. Full care is an add-on when you book and includes what full care includes.

Can two horses share a stall?

No, it’s one horse per stall. Book one stall for each horse you’re bringing.

Is there room for my trailer or RV?

Yes, but spots are limited. Add parking when you book a stall, or call Jenna at (305) 409-9656 to book it on its own.

Can I see the stall before I book?

Yes. Every stall on the ranch map has its photos, size and price. You’re also welcome to call Jenna and come see it.

Will I get updates on my horse?

Yes. Owners with a horse at the ranch get a login to the owner portal, where the team posts photos and videos of your horse.

How can I pay?

Credit card, Zelle, cash or check.

Do you offer monthly boarding?

Yes, full-service monthly boarding. Contact us to reserve a stall.
